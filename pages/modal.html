MODAL·SUCCESS

Success / Reward Modal

Highly visual confirmation overlay used for major milestones (rent paid, lease signed, KYC verified).

Payments
🎉

Rent Paid Successfully!

Your payment of ₹25,000 for October has been transferred to Vikram Malhotra.

You Earned
🪙 500 Points
Behavioral & Design Rationale
  • Glassmorphism Backdrop: backdrop-filter: blur(8px) focuses attention entirely on the modal while keeping context of where the user is. Reduces cognitive load.
  • Center Alignment: Success messages read best centered. It feels celebratory and balanced (Apple HIG pattern).
  • Reward Injection: Immediately showing earned points within the success modal reinforces the habit loop (B.J. Fogg behavior model).
  • Vertical Button Stacking: Mobile thumb reach is optimized when primary and secondary actions are stacked vertically rather than horizontally. Primary is on top.
MODAL·CONFIRM

Destructive Action Modal

High-friction interruption for irreversible actions like evictions, lease cancellations, or deleting properties.

Property Settings
⚠️

Remove Tenant?

This will immediately terminate the digital lease for Priya Sharma and stop all automated rent collections.

This action cannot be undone.
Behavioral & Design Rationale
  • Left Alignment: Destructive modals are left-aligned for better reading comprehension. Reading risk factors requires analytical processing, not celebratory scanning.
  • No "X" Close Button: Removing the top-right close button forces the user to make a deliberate choice between 'Cancel' and 'Remove'. Increases friction on purpose.
  • Color Psychology: Red icon and border trigger innate danger associations.
  • Button Placement: 'Cancel' on the left (safe), 'Remove' on the right (progress/action). Destructive action is explicitly colored red.
MODAL·FORM

Data Entry / Form Modal

Focused data collection overlay. Prevents users from losing context by navigating to a new page.

Team

Invite Co-Owner

They will have full access to view payments and manage tenants.

Behavioral & Design Rationale
  • Context Retention: Using a modal for 1-3 inputs (instead of a page route) keeps the user anchored. Conversion drops by 20% when routing away for simple tasks.
  • Input Styling: Inputs have a slight inset background var(--color-surface-inset) to contrast against the modal surface, making the tap targets clear.
  • Single CTA: The footer contains a single, full-width primary button. The 'Cancel' action is handled by the top-right 'X' or tapping the backdrop, optimizing screen real estate.

MODAL·SHEET

Bottom Sheet Action Menu

Ergonomic mobile pattern for displaying complex options, filters, or sharing sheets.

Properties

Behavioral & Design Rationale
  • Thumb Ergonomics (Fitts's Law): Bottom sheets place all interactive elements in the "Thumb Zone," making them 40% faster to interact with on modern tall smartphones compared to centered modals.
  • Drag Handle: The pill shape at the top visually affords swiping down to dismiss, matching native iOS and Android paradigms.
  • High Border Radius: 32px top radius creates a softer, more organic feel that slides up smoothly, reinforcing the premium Glass Brutalism aesthetic of Rentenpe.
