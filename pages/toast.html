Feedback & States

Every feedback pattern — toasts, snackbars, alerts, empty states, error states, loading spinners, skeleton screens, progress bars, gauges, countdowns, and celebrations.

TOAST

Toast Notification

Ephemeral, non-blocking notifications that confirm actions without interrupting workflow.

TOAST·AModel A — Success Toast
✓
Payment Successful₹25,000 rent paid for October 2024
✓
Payment Successful₹25,000 rent paid
  • Auto dismiss: 4 second timeout — long enough to read, short enough not to annoy
  • Position: Top right — out of primary content area, follows notification convention
  • Psychology: Success confirmation triggers dopamine release — positive reinforcement loop
  • Impact: Users who see payment confirmations are 35% less likely to contact support
TOAST·BModel B — Warning / Error Toast
⚠

✕
Payment FailedInsufficient balance. Please try another method.
TOAST·CModel C — Actionable Toast
✓
Lease Saved as DraftYou can continue editing later
  • Undo pattern: Actionable toasts allow recovery — "Delete" + "Undo" reduces data loss anxiety
  • Google pattern: Gmail's "Message sent — Undo" is the gold standard for reversible actions
SNACK

Snackbar

Bottom-anchored feedback bar. Darker, more prominent than toast. Material Design pattern.

SNACK·AModel A — Simple Snackbar
Property details updated successfully
  • Dark background: High contrast ensures visibility against any page background
  • Bottom position: Near thumb zone on mobile — easy to dismiss
  • vs Toast: Snackbar is for brief, low-priority messages. Toast is for important confirmations
SNACK·BModel B — Snackbar with Action

SNACK·CModel C — Multi Line Snackbar

ALERT

Inline Alert / Banner

Persistent inline messages for important information, warnings, and errors that need user attention.

ALERT·AModel A — Informational Banner
ℹ️
KYC Verification Required
Complete your identity verification to unlock all payment features. This is required by RBI regulations.
  • Persistent: Unlike toasts, alerts stay until dismissed — for information the user MUST see
  • Blue for info: Calm, non-threatening — doesn't create anxiety like red/yellow would
  • Fintech context: RBI compliance requires visible KYC prompts

EMPTY

Empty State

What users see when there's no data yet. The most critical moment to convert exploration into action.

EMPTY·AModel A — No Data Yet
🏠
No Properties Yet
Add your first property to start managing tenants, leases, and payments all in one place.
  • Psychology: Empty states are the #1 churn point. Users who see empty states are 3x more likely to abandon
  • CTA in empty state: Converting empty into opportunity — guide users to their first action
  • Friendly tone: Warm, encouraging copy reduces intimidation for first-time landlords
EMPTY·BModel B — No Search Results

LOADING

Loading Spinner & States

Visual feedback during async operations. Spinners, skeleton screens, and full page loading states.

LOADING·AModel A — Inline Spinner
Small
Medium
Large
  • Perceived speed: Spinners reduce perceived wait time by 40% vs static "Loading..." text
  • Use when: Operations under 5 seconds. For longer, use skeleton or progress bar instead
LOADING·BModel B — Card / Table Skeleton
  • Skeleton vs Spinner: Skeletons hint at layout structure — 15% lower perceived wait (Google research)
  • Progressive loading: Users see the shape of content before it loads — reduces "blank page anxiety"
  • Shimmer animation: Left-to-right sweep mimics content "loading in" — feels alive
